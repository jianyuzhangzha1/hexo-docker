### WHAT I EXPETCT

We went to Tokyo twice before, as my daughter is a anime enthusiast. People say that the real Japan is in Kansai. I always want to go to Kyoto to experience it. Fortunately, my daughter said that she would go to Universal Studios to see Harry Potter, which put Kansai execution on the agenda.

I originally planned to go at the end of June to avoid the peak flow of people. When making plans, it was discovered that the important festival in Kyoto is on July 17th. To experience the cultural characteristics, it is still not too early. Universal Studios' quick pass needs to be booked two months in advance, so many things can be prepared. Hotels, airline tickets, itinerary, cultural background to be prepared in advance, information about the people of Du Lai, Tang Zhaoti Temple, Qin Jiugong Temple, as well as food and shopping.
